# App Store copy: PaceFlow Reader

Status: launch draft for iOS. Working brand only. `Paceflow` is already used by an active Google Play running app and other software services, so confirm trademark, domain and App Store Connect availability—or select a more distinctive final name—before submission.

Apple's current limits are 30 characters for Name, 30 for Subtitle, 170 for Promotional Text, 4,000 for Description and 100 bytes for Keywords. Sources: [App information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/app-information) and [Platform version information](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/platform-version-information).

The copy deliberately avoids guaranteed speed/comprehension and medical claims. Remove any feature from the final metadata if it is not present and testable in the submitted build.

### Description

```text
Reading at a fixed pace can be wonderfully focused — until the rhythm feels wrong, a long word runs off the screen, or an app forgets where you stopped.

PaceFlow Reader is a calm, offline pace reader for real books. Import your own DRM-free ebooks and documents, choose a comfortable speed, and keep your eyes on one clear reading point. No account is required.

READ IN YOUR RHYTHM

• Display one word at a time with an optional Optimal Recognition Point (ORP)
• Try smart two-word flow: only short word pairs are grouped, so the screen stays easy to read
• Tune pauses separately for commas, sentence endings and other punctuation
• Change WPM while reading and see both your selected speed and effective pace
• Long words automatically shrink just enough to fit your screen

PAUSE WITHOUT LOSING THE THOUGHT

Pause to see the current word inside its surrounding text. More space is reserved for what you have just read, while the next passage stays visible for orientation. Continue or open the full text and tap an earlier word whenever you need more context.

MADE FOR FINISHING BOOKS

• Keep EPUB, FB2, DOCX and TXT files in your personal library
• Continue from the exact reading position after closing the app
• Navigate supported books with a table of contents
• Search the full text and jump between results
• Save bookmarks with a nearby text preview
• See progress and estimated time remaining

COMFORTABLE, ONE-HANDED CONTROL

Adjust the word size, appearance and focus color. Start or pause with a tap, or use the Space key when a hardware keyboard is connected. iOS volume keys always keep their system function.

PRIVATE. LOCAL. OFFLINE.

Your imported books, reading position, bookmarks and settings stay on your device. Native v1 has no account, cloud sync, analytics or tracking. The reader works without an internet connection.

SUPPORTED CONTENT

• DRM-free EPUB and FB2 ebooks
• DOCX and TXT documents
• Text pasted directly into the app

DRM-protected ebooks are not supported. Reading speed and comprehension vary by person and material. Start at a pace that feels comfortable, slow down for difficult passages, and return to earlier text whenever you need more context.
```

## Pre-submission truthfulness checklist

- Confirm every listed format against production fixtures, not only extension detection.
- Confirm the native target contains no enabled sync endpoint, account flow, analytics SDK or tracking SDK.
- Confirm App Privacy labels and the public privacy policy say that imported books and reading history are not collected.
- Confirm the iOS External Media Controls switch remains unavailable, Space works with a hardware keyboard, and volume keys retain their system behavior.
- Confirm TOC fallback behavior for EPUB/FB2/DOCX without structural headings.
- Confirm that `effective pace`, time remaining and two-word counting use the shipped calculation.
- If StoreKit is added in a future build, confirm purchase restoration on a fresh sandbox account and the intended Family Sharing configuration before mentioning it in metadata.
- Remove all bracketed review-note placeholders.
- Provide live Privacy Policy and Support URLs. A Privacy Policy URL is required for iOS.
