Chapter One: A Quiet Morning

The first light reached the kitchen table before the city fully woke. Mira set down a cup of tea, opened the window, and listened to the small sounds outside: a bicycle bell, a closing gate, and sparrows gathering beneath the trees.

She had promised herself ten unhurried minutes before the day began. There was no target to beat and no message waiting for an instant reply. She simply followed the sentence in front of her, one phrase at a time.

Chapter Two: Finding a Rhythm

Some passages moved easily. Others asked for a pause. A short sentence could carry a surprising amount of meaning, while a longer one became clear only after she looked back at its beginning.

Mira changed the pace until it felt comfortable. When an unfamiliar word appeared, she stopped and let the surrounding paragraph return. The earlier lines reminded her how the idea had started; the next few words showed where it was going.

Chapter Three: Keeping Your Place

Later, a delivery arrived and the morning became busy. Mira closed the reader without making a note of the page. In the evening, she opened it again and found the same passage waiting for her.

The point was not to rush. The useful rhythm was the one that helped her stay present, notice when attention wandered, and return without losing the thread.
